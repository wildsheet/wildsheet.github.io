Regular Meeting Every 2 months

Wildsheet General Meetings - Slack

Our regular meeting happens every 2 months, we usually discuss one or two original research articles or methods papers, new products, upcoming workshops and conferences.

The online meeting details are provided on the Slack, join us there to keep up-to-date with our General Meetings.

Symopsium 22nd April 2024

Light-sheet in Action - Francis Crick Institute

This 1-day symposium aims to form new connections and collaborations between biologists, microscopists, image analysts and optical engineers, providing an opportunity to inform each other about current and advanced applications using light-sheet microscopy. The discussion will be focused on the benefits of this technology without ignoring its limits and exploring possible alternative or complementary imaging approaches.

The symposium is aimed at all prospective and existing scientists using light-sheet microscopy, from those thinking of using light-sheet microscopy for the first time to the established researchers using light-sheet routinely and actively developing methodology and technology.
