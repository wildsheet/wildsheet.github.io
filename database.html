Tissue Clearing Database

A non-exhaustive, regularly-updated database of tissue clearing methods with filterable parameters and links to papers that use the technique, alongside a discussion space for users (GitHub login required).

DISCLAIMER: Gemini AI models were used to extract this data from tissue clearing reviews. There may be errors, and certainly omissions such as incomplete metadata. We mostly rely on abstracts for real-world application papers. Please check the original sources for confirmation.
